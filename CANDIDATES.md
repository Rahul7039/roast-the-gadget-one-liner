# Candidates — One-Line Roast: The Gadget You Use Every Day

Gate: one original line, 1–200 characters, roasting a gadget or app used every
day. Judge weighs, in order: names a daily gadget/app, actually funny on first
read, fresh angle, neatly worded, one person's specific gripe with a concrete
detail. Scored below against that brief.

## 1. Laptop fan — 146 chars
> My laptop's fan starts screaming like a hairdryer in a panic attack every time I open a spreadsheet — two cells in and it's preparing for takeoff.
**Verdict:** Funny image, but "laptop fan sounds like a jet engine" is one of the most reheated laptop complaints there is — fails freshness (criterion 3).

## 2. Earbuds Bluetooth — 120 chars
> My earbuds drop out mid-sentence so often I've started saying goodbye twice — once to the person, once to the Bluetooth.
**Verdict:** Relatable and tight, but Bluetooth dropouts are the obvious earbud complaint — fresh angle is thin, and "saying goodbye twice" is a known gag shape.

## 3. Washing machine jingle — 122 chars ★ CHOSEN
> My washing machine plays a triumphant little jingle when it finishes — for one sock. One job, and it still wants applause.
**Verdict:** Strongest. Names a daily appliance, lands on first read, the victory-jingle-for-one-sock detail is a fresh, specific, real behaviour nobody roasts, and it is tight at 122 chars with a clean two-beat structure (setup, punch).

## 4. Robot vacuum — 120 chars
> My robot vacuum has mapped my whole house and still gets stuck under the same chair every day — a genius with a nemesis.
**Verdict:** Good concrete detail and a nice closing word, but "robot vacuum gets stuck" is a familiar trope — less fresh than the washing-machine jingle.

## 5. Smartwatch standing — 120 chars
> My smartwatch congratulates me for standing up, like sitting was the achievement — a coach that applauds me for gravity.
**Verdict:** Neat and specific, but the "watch applauds trivial activity" angle is well worn, and the punch is softer than #3's.

## 6. Smart TV menu — 120 chars
> My smart TV takes longer to open its own menu than the film takes to disappoint me — a cinema that buffers the curtains.
**Verdict:** Tight with a good final image, but buffering jokes about smart TVs are common, and the roast splits its target between the TV and the film.

## Choice
Candidate 3 — the washing machine. It is the only one whose angle (a machine
demanding applause for one sock) is genuinely fresh while staying a concrete,
daily, first-read-funny gripe.
